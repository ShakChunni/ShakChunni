## About

I am a cybersecurity researcher and research-focused engineer working at the intersection of **cryptography, networked systems, and machine learning**. My current work is centered on experimentally evaluating security mechanisms under realistic system and network constraints, then using those measurements to design and assess practical mitigations.

My software-engineering background helps me turn research questions into reproducible systems: building testbeds, automating experiments, collecting network and application measurements, analysing results, and translating them into research artifacts.

## Current Research

### Post-Quantum Security for IoT & Edge Systems
I am investigating how **post-quantum TLS authentication and key establishment** affect MQTT-based IoT/edge communication under degraded networks. The work studies transport-level effects such as packetisation, loss, TCP retransmissions, connection delay, and repeated reconnect cost, alongside PQC-preserving mitigations including **TLS session resumption and connection-lifecycle management**.

The experimental stack includes **ML-KEM-768**, **ML-DSA-65**, TLS 1.3, MQTT, Docker-based network testbeds, controlled link impairment, packet-level measurement, and statistical analysis.

### Neural Cryptanalysis
I am also exploring **SAT-guided neural cryptanalysis** for lightweight block ciphers, combining neural distinguishers with constraint solving for multi-round key-recovery research. The current direction uses **SPECK32/64** as a research target and focuses on where learned distinguishers begin to lose effectiveness as cipher rounds increase.

### Applied Machine Learning
Alongside security research, I have worked on applied ML systems including **computer vision and accessibility-oriented recognition pipelines**, with an emphasis on turning models into testable end-to-end systems rather than treating model accuracy in isolation.
